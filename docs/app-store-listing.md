## Description

TunedTV is one remote and guide for every TV in your home.

Say what you want to watch. TunedTV finds it and puts it on the right TV, whether that means
changing the channel on your cable box or opening the right streaming app.

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT
• Changes channels on DIRECTV boxes, not just streaming apps.
• Every TV in one app: Roku, LG, and Google TV / Android TV, side by side.
• Room by room: "on the bedroom TV", "on all TVs".
• Knows your lineup: "CNBC is 602 on Fios." Your exact channel number, every time.
• Sports done right: the game, the right local or regional channel for your ZIP, and one tap
  to put it on.
• Shows and movies open on the title in Netflix, Hulu, Disney+, Max, Peacock, Prime Video and
  more, not just the app's home screen.

ASK TUNEDTV
Type or talk: "put on ESPN", "play The Office", "what's a good movie tonight?", "volume up".
Works with Siri too: "Ask TunedTV to put on the Mets game."

ALSO
• Tonight's games for your teams, with channel numbers for your provider.
• Where to stream any movie or show, including free options.
• Alerts when your team plays or a show you follow is back.

Free. No account needed to start.
